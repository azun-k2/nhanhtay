What it is

Nhanh Tay (“quick hands” in Vietnamese) is a fast ordering game. Every question gives you four answers, A to D. Tap them in the right order before the timer runs out: smallest to largest, oldest to newest, first step to last, or the words of a saying.

  • Think while it types. The question types itself out and the answers arrive one by one; the clock starts only when all four are on screen.
  • No lives. A wrong order just waits for you to fix it, but the first time the clock beats you, the game is over. The time gets shorter every ten questions.
  • Two quizzes. 150 questions in Vietnamese and 150 in English, written separately. The questions follow the language you choose in the game.
  • Made for fast fingers. A big tactile pad with haptics, synthesized sounds and music, light and dark, iPhone, iPad and Android tablets.
  • Leaderboard and achievements. Game Center on iPhone and iPad, Google Play Games on Android. Your best score and achievements are kept on your device too, so the game works fully signed out or offline.

iPhone and iPad with iOS and iPadOS 17 or later. Android 8 or later.

Privacy in one paragraph

Nhanh Tay has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account, and collects no data. The only network use is the platform's game service (Game Center or Google Play Games), and only when you are signed in to it. The full privacy policy says the same at more length.
