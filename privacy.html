The short version

Nhanh Tay collects no data. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account.

On your device

Your settings, best score, games played and achievements are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the game sends your score and the achievements you earn to that service, so they can appear on its leaderboard and in your profile. This is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; the game receives only what it needs to show the leaderboard. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. This is the game’s only use of the network.

Sharing

When you share your score, the system share sheet sends the text and score picture only where you choose.
