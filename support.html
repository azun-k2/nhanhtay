How to play

Tap the letters on the pad in the order the question asks for. Each letter goes to the next free slot. Tap a placed letter, or its slot, to send it back. You can also tap the answer rows themselves. When all four slots are in the right order, the answers slide into place and Next appears.

The timer starts only once all four answers are on screen. It is 12 seconds at first and half a second shorter every ten questions, never under 6. The first time it runs out, the game ends.

Questions and answers

The questions are in the wrong language. They follow the game’s own language switch (on the menu and in Settings), not the phone’s. Vietnamese and English have their own questions.

No sound. Sound effects follow the silent switch on iPhone. Music and sound effects each have a switch in Settings.

The leaderboard shows only my own score. Sign in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android) to see other players. Everything you earned while signed out is sent when you sign in.

The game paused. If you leave the app during a question, the clock stops and the question is hidden until you tap Continue.

I answered every question. Well done: that is the win. New questions come with updates.
